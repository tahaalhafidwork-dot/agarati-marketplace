# AGARATİ MVP study

Stub. Notes from product framing.

## Positioning

AGARATİ.COM is a **focused Iraqi real-estate marketplace** — not a general classifieds platform. Arabic-first chrome, mobile-first, with listings, residential projects, and approved agencies.

## What “focused” means

- No daily rental in v1
- Agencies are an account type, not a public CRM
- Contact is lead-based (`ContactLead`), not chat
- Exact addresses stay off the public page
- Favorites must ship before calling the MVP launched
- Listings go through admin moderation

## Sprint 0 takeaway

Prove the information architecture and the public shell. Do not simulate a backend.
